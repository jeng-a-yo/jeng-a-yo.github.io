About

01 / Background

I am a Mathematics student at National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), exploring the connections between combinatorial optimization and computer vision. My research centers on QUBO formulations and graph partitioning, with geometric detection as a way to connect mathematical models to visual data. I am a 2025 NSTC Undergraduate Research Project grantee.
